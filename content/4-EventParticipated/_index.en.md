## Overview

During my internship with the First Cloud AI Journey program, I participated in three technology events covering cloud architecture, artificial intelligence and modern software engineering. These activities complemented my internship project and provided opportunities to explore practical engineering approaches and learn from the AWS community.

### [Event 1 – Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels](4.1-event1/)

**Date and time:** October 2, 2026, 8:30 AM–12:00 PM (GMT+7)  
**Location:** Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

The session connected cloud and AI technologies with distributed system design, reliability, innovation and professional development. Its key takeaway was the importance of designing software around both technical requirements and long-term operational needs.

[View Event 1](4.1-event1/)

---

### [Event 2 – Cloud Mastery 1](4.2-event2/)

**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

The event explored security, scalability, reliability and cost in cloud-based AI applications. It introduced AWS Lambda, Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore, and examined model selection and trustworthy AI through the Cox Automotive case study.

[View Event 2](4.2-event2/)

### [Event 3 – AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026 – HCMC Watch Party](4.3-event3/)

**Date and time:** September 29, 2026, 9:00 AM (GMT+7)  
**Location:** AWS Office, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

The watch party connected the Ho Chi Minh City community to the main event in Hanoi, featuring discussions on cloud technology, generative and agentic AI, and modern application development.

[View Event 3](4.3-event3/)

## Overall Reflection

Together, these events reinforced the importance of combining AI knowledge with sound cloud architecture, responsible resource usage and continuous learning. The topics are relevant to my Driver Drowsiness & Distraction Detection project, especially the separation of local AI processing from cloud services, data protection and operational reliability.
