## 2. Event Objectives

The meeting offered students and the engineering community an opportunity to explore industry perspectives on Cloud Computing and AI. Its focus connected software design, the operation of large-scale systems and the ability of engineering teams to innovate.

## 3. Main Activities and Highlights

### 3.1. Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence

Cloud infrastructure provides flexible resources for developing digital services, while AI creates new opportunities for automation and practical problem-solving.

### 3.2. Building Systems at Global Scale

Distributed architectures introduce requirements for scalability, reliability and availability. These considerations need to be evaluated alongside the needs of the application.

### 3.3. Innovation and Career Development

The program addressed problem-solving, adaptation to emerging technologies and the importance of continuous learning in an engineering career.

### 3.4. Q&A and Community Networking

The Q&A and networking sessions provided opportunities for exchange among learners, developers and technology professionals.

## 4. Knowledge and Lessons Learned

- System design should balance performance, stability and maintainability rather than focusing only on functionality.
- AI adoption should address specific user needs.
- Architectural knowledge and analytical skills complement programming expertise.

## 5. Relevance to the Internship Project

For the **Driver Drowsiness & Distraction Detection** project, these lessons can be applied in the following ways:

| Lesson | Project application |
|---|---|
| Separation of responsibilities | Edge AI processes images on the device; AWS receives event metadata. |
| Reliability | Local buffering, retries and event identifiers help reduce data loss and duplicate records. |
| Scalability | API Gateway, Lambda and DynamoDB are separated into components with distinct responsibilities. |
| Security and privacy | Video remains on the device, IAM permissions are restricted, and Cognito controls dashboard access. |
| Operational visibility | CloudWatch provides monitoring for errors and system metrics. |

## 6. Conclusion

The event provided a broader perspective on careers in Cloud and AI engineering. Its practical value for the internship lies in connecting architectural decisions with product quality and objectives.
