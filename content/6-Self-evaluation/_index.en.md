### Internship Overview

During my ten weeks in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program at AWS, I expanded my knowledge of cloud computing and applied my Artificial Intelligence studies at FPT University to the development of a practical system. My learning progressed from AWS fundamentals, security, networking, storage, and computing to designing and deploying an application that combines AI and cloud technologies.

The **Driver Drowsiness & Distraction Detection** project was a major outcome of my internship. I developed a system to detect signs of driver drowsiness and distraction using OpenCV, MediaPipe, and features such as EAR, MAR, and Head Pose. The system processes images on an edge device, sends event metadata to AWS, and provides a dashboard for reviewing event history. Through this project, I gained a better understanding of how to integrate computer vision with serverless architecture, manage access permissions, protect data, and consider operating costs.

In addition to the project, maintaining a worklog and collaborating on three technical blog articles helped me consolidate my knowledge, improve my ability to read English technical documentation, and communicate technical concepts in both languages.
