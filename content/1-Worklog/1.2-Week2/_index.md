### Week 2 Objectives:

* Understand the basic principles of identity management and access control on AWS.
* Study AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM), including Users, Groups, Roles, and Policies.
* Understand the principle of least privilege and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
* Review networking fundamentals, including IP addressing, CIDR notation, and subnetting.
* Explore Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) and its components.
* Understand Public Subnets, Private Subnets, Route Tables, Internet Gateways, and NAT Gateways.
* Learn how Security Groups and Network Access Control Lists (NACLs) protect cloud resources.

### Week 2 Achievements:

* Developed an understanding of AWS IAM and its role in controlling access to cloud resources.
* Learned the differences between:
  * IAM Users and User Groups.
  * IAM Roles and IAM Policies.
  * Identity-based and resource-based permissions.
* Understood the importance of least-privilege permissions, temporary credentials, and MFA.
* Studied the structure of IAM policy documents and basic access control scenarios.
* Gained knowledge of Amazon VPC and virtual network isolation.
* Learned the purpose of important VPC components:
  * Public and Private Subnets.
  * Route Tables.
  * Internet Gateway.
  * NAT Gateway.
  * Security Groups.
  * Network ACLs.
* Understood how Security Groups and NACLs differ in their behavior and scope.
* Improved the ability to analyze AWS architecture diagrams and basic network connectivity requirements.
* Established a foundation for deploying EC2 Instances and other AWS resources securely.
